# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Prove to be a project where anyone can take part without harassment,
whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Criticise work, not people. A wrong verdict is a bug, not a failing of whoever found
  or caused it.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion on the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Using Prove's findings to attack a named person or organisation in this project's
  spaces. Describe the discrepancy, never the author or a motive.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that
break this code, and may ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate.

Report a problem by opening a confidential security advisory on the repository or by
contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are reviewed promptly
and kept private.
